# Faithful to the King

## Stewarding Everything God Has Entrusted to Us

> Everything belongs to the King.  
> We are stewards of what He entrusts.  
> Faithfulness is our responsibility.

### About the Book

*Faithful to the King* is a short faith-based book about biblical stewardship and Kingdom responsibility.

Its central message is simple:

**God is the King and Owner. We are stewards, not owners.**

Everything God places in our hands—our time, resources, abilities, opportunities, relationships, influence, and responsibilities—is entrusted to us for a purpose.

This book explores what it means to remain faithful to the King with whatever He has entrusted to us.

### Biblical Foundation

> “Now, a person who is put in charge as a manager must be faithful.”
>
> — 1 Corinthians 4:2

### Core Principle

**The King owns everything.  
The King entrusts.  
The steward manages.  
The steward gives an account.**
