# Faithful to the King — Manuscript

## Working Title

**Faithful to the King**

### Subtitle

**Stewarding Everything God Has Entrusted to Us**

---

## Manuscript Purpose

This manuscript explores biblical stewardship through a Kingdom perspective:

**God is the King and Owner.  
We are stewards of what He entrusts.  
Faithfulness is our responsibility.**

---

## Manuscript Structure

### Part I — The King
Understanding who owns everything.

### Part II — The Steward
Understanding our identity and responsibility.

### Part III — The King's Trust
Understanding what God entrusts to us.

### Part IV — The Test of Faithfulness
Learning to steward faithfully in every season.

### Part V — Returning to the King
Understanding accountability, reward, and finishing faithfully.

---

## Biblical Foundation

**1 Corinthians 4:2**

> “Now, a person who is put in charge as a manager must be faithful.”
