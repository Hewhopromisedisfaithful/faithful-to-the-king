# CHAPTER 40 ## FAITHFUL TO THE KING > **“Now, a person who is put in charge as a manager must be faithful.”** > — 1 Corinthians 4:2 There is a question that has been quietly standing behind every chapter of this book. It is not merely: **What have I received?** It is not: **How much have I accomplished?** It is not even: **How much have I multiplied?** The deeper question is: **How faithful have I been with what the King placed in my hands?** That question reaches beyond money. It reaches beyond position, success, influence, possessions, business, ministry, opportunities, relationships, talents, time, and resources. It reaches the heart. Because stewardship has never been primarily about what we possess. It has always been about **how we respond to the One who entrusted it to us.** We began this journey with a foundational truth: **The King owns everything.** “The earth is the LORD’s, and everything in it.” — Psalm 24:1 Everything begins there. Before there was a resource in our hands, there was a King who owned it. Before there was an opportunity, there was a King who opened the door. Before there was a gift, there was a King who gave it. Before there was a position, there was a King who permitted us to occupy it. Before there was increase, there was a King who provided the seed. And before there was a calling, there was a King who called. We are not owners pretending to serve. We are servants entrusted with what belongs to the King. That changes everything. --- ## THE KING WAS ALWAYS THE CENTER Perhaps the greatest danger in stewardship is forgetting who the center is. A steward can become so focused on the assignment that he forgets the Master. He can become so focused on the blessing that he forgets the Blesser. He can become so focused on the work that he forgets the One who gave him the work. He can become so concerned about building something that he forgets **Whose kingdom he is building.** But faithful stewardship always brings the heart back to the King. **The King is the center.** Not our reputation. Not our applause. Not our success. Not our comfort. Not our legacy. Not even our own dreams. The King. This is why stewardship begins with surrender. Before asking God, “What can You give me?” the steward learns to ask, **“Lord, what would You have me do with what You have already placed in my hands?”** That is a different question. And sometimes it leads somewhere we expected. Sometimes it leads somewhere we never imagined. Sometimes it requires patience. Sometimes obedience. Sometimes discipline. Sometimes sacrifice. Sometimes waiting. Sometimes letting go. Sometimes beginning again. But wherever the King leads, the faithful steward learns to say: **“It belongs to You. I belong to You. Therefore, I will be faithful.”** --- ## EVERYTHING ENTRUSTED HAS PURPOSE Throughout this journey, we have looked at many areas of stewardship. Our hearts. Our wisdom. Our obedience. Our discipline. Our responsibility. Our integrity. Our contentment. Our generosity. Our service. Our endurance. Our multiplication. None of these stand alone. They are different expressions of the same calling: **To faithfully manage what belongs to the King.** If God gives us money, we steward it. If He gives us time, we steward it. If He gives us influence, we steward it. If He gives us relationships, we steward them. If He gives us talent, we develop it. If He gives us authority, we use it responsibly. If He gives us opportunity, we respond with wisdom. If He gives us a business, we operate it with integrity. If He gives us people to lead, we serve them. If He gives us a season of waiting, we remain faithful in it. If He gives us little, we do not despise it. If He gives us much, we do not become entitled to it. **Everything entrusted has purpose.** And everything entrusted carries responsibility. The question is never simply whether we received something. The question is what we did with it. --- ## FAITHFULNESS IS NOT PERFECTION There is something important the steward must understand. Faithfulness does not mean never making a mistake. It does not mean never becoming tired. It does not mean never experiencing disappointment. It does not mean every decision will be perfect. It does not mean every season will produce visible results. Faithfulness means that when we fall, we return. When we fail, we repent. When we become weak, we seek the King again. When we lose direction, we listen again. When we are corrected, we humble ourselves. When the fruit is delayed, we continue to trust. When the assignment becomes difficult, we do not immediately abandon it. The faithful steward is not the person who never struggles. The faithful steward is the person who continues to place his life under the authority of the King. There may be tears. There may be delays. There may be mistakes. There may be seasons when we do not understand what the King is doing. But faithfulness says: **“I may not understand everything, but I will not forget who You are.”** --- ## THE STEWARD DOES NOT HAVE TO BE THE MASTER One of the greatest freedoms of stewardship is realizing that we were never called to carry the weight of ownership. The King carries that. We carry faithfulness. The King determines the ultimate outcome. We carry obedience. The King gives the increase. We faithfully plant and water. The King sees the whole story. We faithfully walk the portion placed before us. This is why comparison is unnecessary. Another steward may have received five talents. Another may have received two. Another may have received one. The issue is not whether our assignment looks like someone else's. The issue is whether we are faithful with ours. Perhaps your assignment seems small. Be faithful. Perhaps your responsibility has increased. Be faithful. Perhaps your season is hidden. Be faithful. Perhaps your work is visible to many. Be faithful. Perhaps nobody seems to notice. Be faithful. Because the value of stewardship is not determined by how many people are watching. **The King is watching.** And the King knows. --- ## WHAT WILL REMAIN? One day, many things we currently consider important will no longer matter. Titles will disappear. Possessions will remain behind. Money will change hands. Buildings will eventually decay. Positions will pass to others. Achievements will become history. Even our names may eventually be forgotten by generations that never knew us. But faithfulness before God is never wasted. What we do for the King has eternal significance. This does not mean that our earthly responsibilities are meaningless. Quite the opposite. It means they matter because of **Whom we serve.** The way we treat people matters. The way we handle money matters. The way we conduct business matters. The way we use influence matters. The way we respond to opportunity matters. The way we handle disappointment matters. The way we care for what God entrusted matters. Our ordinary decisions become acts of worship when they are surrendered to the King. A faithful steward can glorify God in a boardroom. At a marketplace. In a classroom. Inside a home. Behind a desk. In a church. In a business. In a quiet room where nobody else sees. Faithfulness does not require a famous platform. It requires a surrendered heart. --- ## THE KING DOES NOT ASK FOR SOMEONE ELSE'S FAITHFULNESS This is where comparison must finally end. God did not ask you to steward another person's assignment. He asked you to steward yours. You do not need another person's resources to be faithful. You do not need another person's influence. You do not need another person's opportunities. You do not need another person's timeline. You do not need another person's story. You need grace to faithfully walk the story God has entrusted to you. The question is not: **“Why did they receive more?”** The better question is: **“What have I done with what the King placed in my hands?”** That question brings us back to responsibility. It brings us back to gratitude. It brings us back to obedience. It brings us back to the King. --- ## THE FINAL COMMISSION If there is one message this book should leave in the heart of the reader, it is this: **You are a steward.** Your life is not meaningless. Your resources are not meaningless. Your opportunities are not meaningless. Your relationships are not meaningless. Your gifts are not meaningless. Your influence is not meaningless. Your present season is not meaningless. Even the things that seem small can become significant when surrendered to the King. Do not wait for a bigger assignment before becoming faithful. Be faithful now. Do not wait for more resources before becoming generous. Be faithful now. Do not wait for recognition before serving. Be faithful now. Do not wait for perfect circumstances before obeying. Be faithful now. Do not wait until you understand everything before trusting. Be faithful now. The King does not need us to control everything. He calls us to faithfully steward what He has placed within our reach. So work with excellence. Give with generosity. Lead with humility. Speak with integrity. Serve with love. Wait with endurance. Decide with wisdom. Obey with courage. Multiply what can be multiplied. Release what must be released. Repent when you fail. Return when you wander. And through every season, keep your eyes on the King. --- ## ONE DAY, THE STEWARD WILL STAND BEFORE THE KING There will come a day when stewardship will be finished. The accounts will be settled. The assignments will be complete. The opportunities will have passed. The seasons will have ended. And the steward will stand before the King. On that day, the question will not be whether we impressed the world. It will not be whether we accumulated the most. It will not be whether everyone knew our name. The deepest issue will be faithfulness. Paul wrote: > **“Now, a person who is put in charge as a manager must be faithful.”** > — 1 Corinthians 4:2 That is the calling. Not perfection. Not fame. Not comparison. **Faithfulness.** And perhaps the greatest desire of a steward's heart can be expressed in the words of Jesus: > **“Well done, my good and faithful servant.”** > — Matthew 25:21 Those words are worth more than applause from people. More than recognition. More than earthly success. More than anything we could build for ourselves. Because they come from the King. --- ## I AM NOT THE OWNER As this book comes to an end, the truth with which we began must become a declaration we carry forward. I am not the owner. I am the steward. My life belongs to God. My time belongs to God. My resources belong to God. My abilities belong to God. My opportunities belong to God. My relationships belong to God. My influence belongs to God. My work belongs to God. My future belongs to God. Everything I have received has come from His hand. Therefore, I will not live as though everything belongs to me. I will live as one who has been entrusted. I will seek wisdom. I will practice obedience. I will walk in discipline. I will accept responsibility. I will guard my integrity. I will cultivate contentment. I will practice generosity. I will serve others. I will endure through difficult seasons. I will multiply what God has entrusted to me. And when I fail, I will return to Him. When I succeed, I will give Him the glory. When I receive increase, I will remember the Source. When I experience loss, I will remember that the King remains faithful. When I do not understand, I will trust. When I am called to wait, I will remain faithful. And when the assignment is complete, **I will return everything to the King.** --- # A FINAL DECLARATION **I belong to the King.** Everything I have belongs to Him. I am not the owner. I am a steward. What He has placed in my hands is a trust, not a possession to worship. I will not despise small beginnings. I will not be controlled by comparison. I will not waste what God has entrusted to me. I will steward my heart. I will steward my time. I will steward my resources. I will steward my relationships. I will steward my influence. I will steward my gifts. I will steward my opportunities. I will lead with humility. I will work with excellence. I will give with generosity. I will serve with love. I will walk with integrity. I will obey even when obedience is costly. I will endure when the fruit is delayed. I will multiply what can be multiplied. I will remain faithful when nobody is watching. I will remember that the King sees. My success will not become my master. My possessions will not become my identity. My position will not become my god. My influence will not become my throne. The King alone will remain at the center. When I have much, I will be faithful. When I have little, I will be faithful. When I am recognized, I will be faithful. When I am overlooked, I will be faithful. When I understand, I will be faithful. When I do not understand, I will still be faithful. Because my confidence is not in my ability to control the future. My confidence is in the faithfulness of the King. **He entrusted me.** **He will sustain me.** **He will guide me.** **He will correct me.** **He will strengthen me.** And when my stewardship is complete, I will gladly place everything back into His hands. **I am not the owner.** **I am the steward.** **The King is faithful.** **Therefore, I will be faithful to the King.** --- # A FINAL PRAYER **My King and my God,** Thank You for every good thing You have placed in my hands. Thank You for life. Thank You for time. Thank You for relationships. Thank You for opportunities. Thank You for resources. Thank You for gifts. Thank You for responsibilities. Thank You even for the seasons that taught me endurance, humility, wisdom, and dependence upon You. Forgive me for the times I treated what You entrusted to me as though it belonged to me. Forgive me for moments when I sought recognition more than faithfulness. Forgive me when comparison distracted me from my own assignment. Forgive me when fear caused me to bury what You entrusted to me. Teach me to live as a faithful steward. Give me wisdom to manage what You have placed in my hands. Give me discipline to remain consistent. Give me courage to obey. Give me integrity when nobody is watching. Give me contentment when I am tempted to compare. Give me generosity when I am tempted to hold tightly. Give me endurance when the journey becomes long. Give me humility when You increase my responsibility. And give me a heart that always remembers: **You are the King.** Everything comes from You. Everything belongs to You. And everything must ultimately return to You. May my life bring honor to Your name. May my work serve Your purposes. May my resources advance what is good. May my relationships reflect Your love. May my influence point people toward You. May my successes bring You glory. May my failures lead me back to You. And when the day comes that my stewardship is finished, may I stand before You with a heart that can say: **“Lord, I gave You what You placed in my hands. I sought to be faithful.”** May my life not merely be remembered for what I built, but for **Whom I served.** May my legacy not point people to me, but to the King. In everything, **let me be found faithful.** In Jesus' name, **Amen.** --- # EPILOGUE ## THE KING'S STEWARD The journey does not truly end with the final page. The book ends. But stewardship continues. Tomorrow, the King will place something else in your hands. Perhaps it will be a responsibility. Perhaps an opportunity. Perhaps a relationship. Perhaps a challenge. Perhaps a new season. Perhaps something small that seems insignificant. Whatever it is, remember what you have learned. You do not need to own it to honor it. You do not need to understand everything to obey. You do not need to have everything to be faithful. You only need to remember the King. So go forward. Carry the kingdom mindset into your home. Carry it into your work. Carry it into your business. Carry it into your relationships. Carry it into your leadership. Carry it into your waiting. Carry it into your giving. Carry it into your decisions. Carry it into every ordinary day. And when the world asks what you are trying to build, remember: **You are not building your own kingdom.** You are faithfully serving the King. When the world asks what you are trying to possess, remember: **Everything already belongs to Him.** When the world asks what success means, remember: **Faithfulness is greater than appearance.** And when you finally reach the end of your earthly stewardship, may your greatest joy be knowing that you did not merely receive from the King. **You were faithful with what He entrusted.** That is the life of a steward. That is the calling. That is the privilege. That is the responsibility. And that is the message of this book: # **BE FAITHFUL TO THE KING.** **The King owns everything.** **The King entrusts.** **The steward receives.** **The steward serves.** **The steward multiplies.** **The steward remains faithful.** **And one day, the steward returns everything to the King.** **Soli Deo Gloria.** *To God alone be the glory.*
