# PART I — THE KING

## Chapter 5 — The Character of a Faithful Steward

The King does not only look at **what** a steward manages. He also looks at **how** the steward manages it.

A person may have great resources, great opportunities, great abilities, or great influence. But in the Kingdom of God, the size of what we have been given is not the measure of our faithfulness.

The real question is:

**Can the King trust us with what He has placed in our hands?**

Scripture gives us a simple standard:

> “Now, a person who is put in charge as a manager must be faithful.”  
> — 1 Corinthians 4:2

Notice that Paul did not say that a manager must be successful, wealthy, influential, or impressive.

He said **faithful**.

That is the character the King seeks in His stewards.

### 1. A Faithful Steward Has Integrity

Integrity means doing what is right even when nobody is watching.

A steward must remember that although other people may not see everything, **the King sees everything**.

Joseph demonstrated this kind of character when he was entrusted with responsibility in Potiphar's household. Even when he was tempted privately, Joseph refused because he understood that his actions were ultimately accountable to God.

> “How could I do such a wicked thing? It would be a great sin against God.”  
> — Genesis 39:9

Integrity protects the trust that the King has placed in us.

Money must be handled honestly.

Responsibilities must be performed honestly.

Relationships must be treated honorably.

Opportunities must not be abused.

Leadership must not become an opportunity for selfish gain.

A faithful steward does not ask only:

**“Can I get away with this?”**

He asks:

**“Would this honor my King?”**

### 2. A Faithful Steward Is Honest

Faithfulness includes honesty in both great and small things.

Jesus said:

> “If you are faithful in little things, you will be faithful in large ones.”  
> — Luke 16:10

Small compromises can reveal a larger problem in the heart.

If we cannot be trusted with a small amount of money, a small responsibility, a simple assignment, or a private decision, why would the King entrust us with greater things?

Faithfulness is built through repeated obedience in ordinary moments.

The King sees the small things.

The King values the small things.

And the small things reveal the heart of the steward.

### 3. A Faithful Steward Is Humble

Everything we have ultimately came from the King.

Paul asked:

> “What do you have that God hasn’t given you?”  
> — 1 Corinthians 4:7

That question destroys entitlement.

Our abilities came from Him.

Our opportunities came from Him.

Our resources came from Him.

Our influence came from Him.

Even the strength to accomplish something comes from Him.

Therefore, the faithful steward does not boast as though he created everything by himself.

Instead, he gives thanks.

Humility says:

**“King, this is not mine. You entrusted it to me.”**

This changes the way we handle blessings.

Instead of asking, “What can I get from this?”

we begin asking,

**“What does the King want me to do with this?”**

### 4. A Faithful Steward Is Obedient

A steward does not have authority to rewrite the King's instructions.

The steward's responsibility is to carry out the King's will.

Jesus said:

> “If you love me, obey my commandments.”  
> — John 14:15

Obedience is one of the clearest expressions of faithfulness.

Sometimes obedience will require sacrifice.

Sometimes it will require patience.

Sometimes it will require doing something that seems small.

Sometimes it will require saying no to something attractive because it does not agree with the King's will.

But a faithful steward understands:

**The King's instructions are more important than the steward's preferences.**

### 5. A Faithful Steward Is Diligent

Faithfulness is not laziness.

Paul instructed believers:

> “Never be lazy, but work hard and serve the Lord enthusiastically.”  
> — Romans 12:11

What God has entrusted to us deserves our best effort.

If we are given a responsibility, we should take it seriously.

If we are given an opportunity, we should prepare for it.

If we are given a talent, we should develop it.

If we are given people to serve, we should serve them well.

Diligence does not mean that everything will always become successful.

It means that we refuse to neglect what the King has placed in our hands.

### 6. A Faithful Steward Is Trustworthy

Trustworthiness is the foundation of stewardship.

A steward becomes valuable to the King not because of how impressive he looks, but because the King knows He can trust him.

Jesus taught:

> “If you are faithful in little things, you will be faithful in large ones.”  
> — Luke 16:10

This means that faithfulness is proven before promotion.

Before greater responsibility comes greater testing of character.

Before greater influence comes the question of whether we can handle the influence we already have.

Before greater resources come opportunities to demonstrate faithfulness with the resources already entrusted to us.

The King does not merely examine our results.

**He examines our character.**

## Kingdom Principle

**The King entrusts resources to stewards whose character must reflect the trust they have received.**

The goal of stewardship is not simply to accomplish more.

It is to become the kind of steward the King can trust.

Integrity keeps us honest.

Humility keeps us dependent on the King.

Obedience keeps us aligned with His will.

Diligence keeps us responsible.

Trustworthiness keeps us faithful.

These qualities form the character of a faithful steward.

## Heart Check

Ask yourself:

**Can the King trust me with what He has already placed in my hands?**

Can He trust me with money?

Can He trust me with time?

Can He trust me with opportunities?

Can He trust me with relationships?

Can He trust me with influence?

Can He trust me when nobody is watching?

These questions are more important than asking how much we have.

Because stewardship is not primarily about **how much the King gives us**.

It is about **how faithfully we handle what He gives us.**

## Faithful Response

King, everything I have comes from You.

Teach me to become a steward whose character honors You.

Make me honest when nobody is watching.

Make me humble when I receive blessing.

Make me obedient when Your will is different from mine.

Make me diligent when the work is difficult.

Make me trustworthy with every responsibility You place in my hands.

I do not want to be impressive before people while being unfaithful before You.

I want to be found faithful.

Because one day, I will give an account to the King.

**Let my life be a life You can trust.**

## Bible Foundation

- 1 Corinthians 4:2 — Faithfulness is required of stewards.
- Genesis 39:9 — Joseph demonstrates integrity before God.
- Luke 16:10 — Faithfulness in little things reveals faithfulness in greater things.
- 1 Corinthians 4:7 — Everything we have ultimately comes from God.
- John 14:15 — Love for Christ is expressed through obedience.
- Romans 12:11 — Believers are called to diligence and wholehearted service.
- Matthew 25:14–30 — The faithful steward is accountable for what the King entrusts.
